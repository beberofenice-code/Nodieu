# Video Notebook (Android app)

Keep all your videos in one place. Every video gets its own box:

- **Green box**: already published
- **Dashed grey box**: not published yet

## Features
- **+ Add video**: pick any video on your phone. There is **no size limit** (500 MB, 2 GB, anything), because the app links to the video on your phone instead of copying it.
- Title and notes for each video (caption, hashtags, where to post).
- **✓ Mark published** turns the box green and saves the date you posted it.
- Filters: All / Not published / Published, with counters at the top.
- **More ⋯**: edit, move up / move down (arrange your order), remove from notebook.
- Tap the picture to play the video.
- "Remove from notebook" never deletes the video file from your phone.

## Build it with AIDE
1. Download this folder to your phone (GitHub → Code → Download ZIP), then unzip it.
2. Open AIDE, browse to the `VideoNotebook` folder and open it as a project.
3. Tap **Run** (▶). AIDE builds the app and installs it.

Requirements: Android 5.0 or newer. No extra libraries are used.
