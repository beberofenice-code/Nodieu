# Nodieu (Android app)

The Nodieu logo is a tilted neon-blue notebook with a black-and-violet screen and a violet play sign.

A black home screen with premium violet folder cards, and a dark poster-style library for your videos. Published videos get an emerald border; drafts get an amber "Draft" badge.

## Features
- **Folders**: the home screen shows your folders as wide cards with their names. **Tagalog Dub** folders are premium blue, **English Dub** folders are premium red, and **Geography** folders are green with a globe logo (a *Geography* folder is created for you once). Tap one to see its videos; ← goes back. Create with **+ New folder** or the folder button; long-press (or ⋮) a folder to rename, change type, or delete it (its videos move to *Unsorted*, nothing is deleted). Move a video with **Folder** in its detail sheet or *Move to folder* in its quick menu.
- **Poster grid**: 3 columns on phones (4–6 on tablets). The header button switches between poster grid, large grid and list.
- **Bottom navigation**: All / Not Yet / Published, each with a live count.
- **+ button**: add any video from your phone. There is **no size limit** (500 MB, 2 GB…), because the app links to the video instead of copying it.
- **Tap a poster** to open the detail sheet: play the video, see status, platform, planned date, notes, added/posted dates and file size, mark published / not published, edit, share, move earlier/later, open in your video player, or delete.
- **Swipe up on a poster** to open the violet **Platforms** panel: one box per platform. A red ✗ means not uploaded there yet; tap it after you upload and it becomes a green ✓ (with the date). When every platform is ✓, the video is marked published. Add platforms with the chips at the bottom; long-press a row to remove one.
- **Post directly**: every video starts with Facebook and YouTube in its swipe-up panel. Tap **Post** on a row and Nodieu opens that app with the video already attached; when you come back it asks if you posted it and ticks the box.
- **Up next**: following your arranged order, the first unpublished video in each folder gets a violet **Up next** badge. The panel shows *Video 3 of 7* and a **Next video ›** button that jumps to the next one, so you never hunt through your files.
- **Long-press a poster** (or tap its ⋮) for one-second actions: mark published, edit, share, remove.
- **Search** titles, and **sort** by my order, date added, date posted, planned date, name or file size.
- **Undo** after adding, removing, or changing published status.
- **Platform** (TikTok, YouTube, Facebook, Instagram, X, Snapchat, or your own) and **planned posting date** with Today / Tomorrow / Overdue reminders.
- Removing a video never deletes the file from your phone.

## Build it with AIDE
1. Download this folder to your phone (GitHub → Code → Download ZIP), then unzip it.
2. In AIDE, open `VideoNotebook/app` and tap **Open Android App Project**.
3. Tap **Run** (▶). AIDE builds the app and installs it.

Requirements: Android 5.0 or newer. No extra libraries are used.
